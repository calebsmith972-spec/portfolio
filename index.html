ABOUT MR. CALEB

My journey started with a simple question: "How hard can it be to become a designer?" Ten years later, I've realized the answer is "Actually, quite hard, but it's worth it." I love the process of turning a messy idea into a clean, functional digital product.

When I'm not building or designing, I'm probably making weird noises on a computer, enjoying nature, building my body to become a nerd with muscles, or trying to convince my gecko Dexter that I'm the one who runs the house (I'm not).
